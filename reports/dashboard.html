What the simulator can and cannot say

These are results of a reproducible model that assumes how players respond to prices. Randomized treatment groups are meaningful inside this model, but do not tell us what real users would do. A live trial needs actual event instrumentation and independently estimated response parameters.

Price sensitivity lab

This is NOT a real price optimizer. It evaluates six hypothetical prices under three different assumed price-response sensitivities. The preferred price may reverse when the assumed elasticity changes.

Scenario assumptions behind this chart

Player activity is held as a common exposure proxy. A formula varies daily purchase probability using the assumed price exponent. These curves are not estimated from observed customers or an actual six-arm experiment.

Integrity & limitations

What is implemented

  • Randomized A/B allocation, one changed treatment variable
  • Event milestones, daily activity, organic IAP and limited-time bundle IAP
  • 292,201 synthetic event log records
  • SQLite analysis with six queries
  • User-level uncertainty and decision gates
  • Revenue reconciliation and automated tests

What is not established

  • No real players, true causal effect or revenue forecast
  • No calibrated demand elasticity or live A/B test
  • No ad revenue, refunds, taxes, UA costs or geographic effects
  • No true LTV model: 21-day RPU is a bounded window
  • No model-driven retention response to price
  • Bootstrap intervals do not solve external validity
Experiment eligibility is at event launch, not after install. Never label exact-day event return as ordinary install-cohort D7 retention.
